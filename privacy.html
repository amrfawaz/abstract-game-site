Abstract — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Abstract ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Amr Fawaz. This policy explains what information the app handles.

The short version

Abstract does not ask for your name, email or any account, and it never sells data. It uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played, such as which levels are hard, using an anonymous app-instance ID. The app shows no ads and doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your own device: which levels are unlocked, your stars and your best score per level. Your saved progress isn't backed up or uploaded anywhere, and deleting the app removes it. Individual level results are also sent as analytics events, described below.

Analytics

The app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google. It collects:

  • Gameplay events: levels started, completed, failed or restarted, hints used, and your moves, stars and points on a level.
  • Screens viewed in the app and basic app-usage information, such as session starts and app version.
  • Device and app-instance identifiers: a random ID generated for this installation of the app. It isn't linked to your name or contact details.
  • Technical information: device model, operating system version, language, and an approximate region derived from your IP address.

This information is used only to improve the game. It is not linked to your identity, not used for advertising, not shared with data brokers and not used to track you across apps or websites. The app does not access your device's advertising identifier (IDFA). Google processes this data under its own terms; see Google's Firebase privacy information.

Children

Abstract is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Permissions

The app doesn't ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or any other protected data.
