Abstract — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Abstract ("the app") is a puzzle game made by Amr Fawaz. This policy explains what information the app handles.

The short version

Abstract does not ask for, collect or sell your personal information. You don't need an account to play, the app shows no ads, and it doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress on your own device: which levels are unlocked, your stars and your best score per level. This data never leaves your device through the app, and deleting the app removes it.

Third-party services

The app includes Google's Firebase SDK (Firebase Core). It can send limited technical diagnostic information to Google, such as a random app-installation identifier, the app version and the device's operating system. Google uses this to provide and maintain its services. This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for advertising or tracking. Learn more in Google's Firebase privacy information.

Children

Abstract is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Permissions

The app doesn't ask for access to your contacts, photos, location, camera, microphone or any other protected data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
