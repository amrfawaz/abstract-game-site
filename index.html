Thanks for playing Abstract!

How to play

Drag any ring around the center to rotate it. A level is solved when every wedge of the circle shows a single color. Later chapters add twists, and each level shows its own rule under the title:

  • Linked: turning a ring also turns its neighbor the opposite way.
  • Cascade: turning a ring drags every ring outside it along.
  • Anchored: the pinned ring can't move, so match everything else to it.
  • Max N: a single move can turn a ring at most N wedges.
  • Move limit: solve within the allowed number of moves.
  • Timed: solve before the clock runs out.
  • Repeated colors: several line-ups count as solved.

Stars and points

Solve a level in par (the fewest possible moves) without hints to earn 3 stars. Every extra move or hint lowers the score. You can replay any unlocked level to improve your result.

Hints, undo and restart

Each level gives you 3 hints that show the next best move. Undo takes back your last move. Restart starts the level again with a new scramble.

Frequently asked questions

Is my progress backed up? Progress is saved on your device only. Deleting the app deletes it.

Does the app need the internet? No, you can play completely offline.

Are there ads or purchases? No.
